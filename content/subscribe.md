Subscribe to a series and new shiurim arrive in your podcast app automatically, as they are published. It is free, and no account is needed on this site.

## What you need

1. A podcast app — Apple Podcasts, or any other; most are free.
2. The series' **feed address**. On any series page, click **Copy feed URL** (there is one for the audio feed and one for the video feed). On many devices the **Subscribe** button copies the address for you.

Then tell your app to follow that address — this is a one-time step per series:

## iPhone and iPad — Apple Podcasts

1. Copy the feed address (above).
2. Open the **Podcasts** app, go to **Library**, and tap **⋯** at the top.
3. Choose **Follow a Show by URL…**, paste the address, and tap **Follow**.

Some series are also listed in Apple Podcasts directly — for those, the Subscribe button opens the series there, and one tap on **Follow** is all it takes.

## Mac — Apple Podcasts

1. Copy the feed address.
2. Open the **Podcasts** app.
3. In the **File** menu, choose **Follow a Show by URL…**, paste the address, and confirm.

## Android

Tap **Subscribe** on the series page and pick your podcast app — or copy the feed address and use the app's **Add by URL** option (usually under Search or the **+** button).

## Windows

Copy the feed address and paste it into your podcast app's **Add by URL** / **Follow a Show by URL** option. In **YouTube Music** (in a browser): **Library → Podcasts → Add podcast → Add by RSS feed**.

## Listening on 24Six?

These shiurim are also carried by 24Six — no subscription steps are needed there; just search for the series in the app.
